About

I work at the intersection of journalism and digital content — where information has to be accurate, engaging and shareable all at once.

Four years in economic journalism, one year in content writing, one year managing social media — I know how to turn complex topics into video that the algorithm pushes well beyond a channel's existing followers. My background spans economics, social sciences and languages, which means I can cover a broad range of subjects with the same depth. Videos I've produced have reached up to 380,000 views.

I work primarily by creating video content for brand channels — not sponsored posts on my own profile. What brands get is a rare combination: editorial accuracy and an instinct for what actually gets watched.
